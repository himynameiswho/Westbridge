Personal guidance grounded in decades of international college counseling experience.

I’m a college counselor, educator, and advisor with more than 30 years of experience helping students and families navigate the complexities of university admissions in the U.S., Canada, the UK, Hong Kong, and beyond.

My career has taken me through international education in China, Japan, Korea, the United Arab Emirates, and the United States, including Shanghai American School. I’m also the author of The Prestige Trap.

My approach is straightforward and student-centered: clear advice, realistic strategy, and a focus on long-term fit, not just a name on a sweatshirt.

How Westbridge works

Students can start at any point. Earlier starts leave more room to explore before decisions narrow.

  1. 1

    Get to know the student

    Grades 9–10

    Interests, strengths, academic plan, and what the family hopes for.

  2. 2

    Explore and shape

    Grade 11

    Testing plan, summer opportunities, campus research, and a first draft of the list.

  3. 3

    Apply

    Grade 12, autumn

    Essays, recommendations, applications, and interviews, on a shared timeline.

  4. 4

    Decide

    Grade 12, spring

    Weighing offers and aid, and preparing for the move to university.
