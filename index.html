The book

The Prestige Trap

An Insider’s Reckoning with the College Admissions Obsession

College admissions has taught families to chase the name on the sweatshirt. Warren’s book asks them to look past status and consider the student’s lived experience: the four years a young person will actually spend, and who they will become there.

The argument comes from decades inside international schools, watching capable students shape themselves around rankings and arrive at universities that impressed everyone but suited no one. It is the conviction Westbridge is built on.

Prestige is a signal, not a fit
A famous name says a great deal about a university’s reputation and very little about whether a particular student will flourish there.
The lived experience is the measure
Teaching style, class size, community and place shape a student’s years far more than a ranking does, so they come first when building a list.
Looking excellent is not the same as being excellent
Students are encouraged to pursue work they care about, not to assemble a résumé built for an admissions reader.

What we help with

Every student works directly with Warren, never handed off to a junior team. He keeps a small caseload so he can track each deadline, read every draft and be available to families when questions come up. Support can be full-process or focused on a single part of the application.

Course and activity planning

Choosing IB, AP or A-Level subjects, and building activities around genuine interests rather than a checklist.

Building the college list

A balanced list across the US, UK, Canada, Hong Kong and beyond, matched to the student's academics, goals and budget.

Essays and personal statements

Helping students find what they want to say and say it in their own voice. Students write; we coach.

Applications and deadlines

Common App, UCAS and school-specific portals, early decision strategy, and a calendar the whole family can follow.

Interview preparation

Practice interviews for alumni, admissions and scholarship conversations, with honest feedback.

Decisions and next steps

Comparing offers, financial aid and waitlist responses, and preparing for the move abroad.
